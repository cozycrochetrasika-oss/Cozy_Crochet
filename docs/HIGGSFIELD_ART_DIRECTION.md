# HIGGSFIELD_ART_DIRECTION.md — Cozy_Crochets 3D & Asset Generation Plan

## 1. Ethical Principle & Product Integrity

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Guardrail**: Higgsfield or any generative AI tool is reserved strictly for decorative brand backgrounds, 3D shader experimentation, and seasonal festival marketing banners.
> **Never replace, modify, or simulate real crochet product photography with AI generation.** The authentic hand-stitches captured in `Bag/`, `Boque/`, `HeadBands/`, `Key_Ring/`, `Kid_Shoe/`, `Rose/`, and `Sunflower/` represent real craftswomen labor and must remain 100% genuine.

---

## 2. Art-Direction Exploration Targets

### 2.1 The "Living Yarn" Hero Concept
- **Visual Subject**: Floating organic spools of warm milk cotton yarn with loose, curving thread fibers casting soft directional ambient shadows.
- **Color Palette Alignment**:
  - Base: Cream (`#FFF8F1`) and Warm Gold (`#CDA567`)
  - Accent: Dusty Rose (`#DFA7AD`) and Soft Sage (`#B8D1BF`)
- **Lighting Mood**: Morning studio light through linen sheer curtains, low specularity, soft diffuse bounce, no harsh synthetic bloom.

### 2.2 Subtle Crochet Flower Ornaments
- Stylized 5-petal crochet motifs and gentle botanical leaves that can be exported as lightweight GLTF/GLB meshes or optimized WebP decorative assets.
- Polygon Budget Target: `< 3,000` triangles per ornament mesh.

### 2.3 Neutral Hero Environment
- Warm neutral studio backdrop incorporating a soft textured linen canvas surface (`#FFFDF9`) with gentle radial vignette (`rgba(73, 54, 48, 0.05)`).
- Provides depth without stealing contrast from foreground interactive elements and headline typography.

### 2.4 Seasonal Festival Banner Templates
- Seasonal holiday themes (Diwali, New Year, Spring Blossom Festival).
- Composition: Natural wooden tray, delicate yarn scissors, skeins of pastel yarn, and gift boxes tied with cotton ribbon.
